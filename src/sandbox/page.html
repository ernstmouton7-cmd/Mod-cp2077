Traffic & driving simulation

Gridlock

A city that drives itself. 110 drivers with their own temper, real signal phasing, police who notice, and a car with every button. Obey the lights, or don't.

  • 23 signalised junctions with protected lefts, pedestrian heads and cameras
  • 8 driver personalities that honk, flash, tailgate, brake-check and ram
  • 42 controls: indicators, hazards, wipers, cruise, clutch, ignition, seatbelt…

WASD drive · Q/E indicators · H horn · Space handbrake · V camera · gamepad and touch supported

Paused

Click a key to rebind it, then press the new key (Esc cancels). Gamepad layout follows the standard Xbox-style mapping.

Signals

Each junction builds its own phase plan from its geometry: opposing approaches share a phase, dedicated left lanes get a leading green arrow, and the flashing yellow arrow means turn after yielding. Actuated signals skip empty phases and extend green while their detectors see cars. Minor junctions switch to flashing at 1–5 am.

Traffic

Drivers follow the Intelligent Driver Model, change lanes when the gain beats their politeness, and plan routes with A*. At junctions they judge the yellow-light dilemma, stop fully at stop signs, take turns at all-way stops, wait in the box to turn left, and won't block the box unless they don't care.

Temper

Cutting someone off, tailgating, honking, high beams, gestures and collisions build anger toward you, scaled by each driver's temper and stress. Past a threshold they honk and flash, then tailgate, brake-check or try to ram you. A wave (G) or a hazard blink can calm them. Police nearby end it.

Law

Red lights, stop signs, speed, wrong-way driving, signalling, headlights, seatbelt and hit-and-run are all detected. Cameras mail fines. An officer who sees it pulls you over; stop at the curb for a ticket, or flee and start a pursuit. Twelve points suspends your license.

Car

Rigid-body model with weight transfer, magic-formula tyres and a friction circle. Without ABS the wheels lock, without traction control they spin. The automatic creeps, parks and reverses; the manual has a clutch and stalls if you lug it.

Engine-agnostic

This simulation is written once and also compiles to Lua, so the same code runs inside the Cyberpunk 2077 mod in this project.
